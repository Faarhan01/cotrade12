How to read this

  • verdict decides everything. noise means do not trade it, whatever the profit column says.
  • win_rate counts every trade; resolved_win_rate only those that reached take-profit or stop-loss. They disagree when the exit rule is loose, and the disagreement is itself the finding.
  • split_check.consistent = false means the profit was all in the first half of the sample. That is not a rule.
  • In the pattern table, edge% has the market's own drift removed and is what the verdict is judged on. raw% mostly measures whether the sample happened to trend.
  • [INVERTED] means the pattern reliably moves the other way, which is a tradeable fact even though it is not the one the pattern claims.
  • An edge that does not replicate across symbols and timeframes is noise, whatever its t-statistic says.
